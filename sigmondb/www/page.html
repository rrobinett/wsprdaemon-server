All known transmitters
Data: the public, read-only tables of sigmondb_public, rebuilt hourly from what fleet stations measure. Transmitter positions come from the HFRNet national station list.
Station positions show the centre of each 6-character grid square, never the exact site. Skywave midpoints derive from those centres.
Stages: 1 known, not heard; 2 heard by at least one station; 3 heard on T6 within ground-wave range; 4 offset confirmed. T6: arrivals timed against a TS-1 PPS pulse injected at the receiver.
"Ground-wave range" and "skywave" go by distance alone for now: within 150 km at 12 MHz and above, 200 km between 6 and 12 MHz, 250 km below 6 MHz. A diurnal stability test will replace this.
