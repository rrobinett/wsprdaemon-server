Fleet board

Which stations send data, and whether each one sends what it usually does. The server judges every station by when its data last arrived, never by the station's own clock.

Data: the public, read-only tables fleet_board and fleet_alarms of sigmondb_public, rebuilt every five minutes. Positions show a 6-character grid square, never the exact site.
Row colour never stands alone: the ON AIR column also says ✓, ✗ with an age, or "no data yet". Grey means no history yet, or a board older than 15 minutes. DEVICES and CAPACITY arrive in a later stage.
